🥞 Fluffy Pancakes

Prep 10 min · Cook 15 min
Servings

Ingredients

    Steps

    1. Whisk flour, sugar, baking powder and salt in a large bowl.
    2. In another bowl, whisk milk, egg and melted butter.
    3. Pour wet into dry and stir until just combined — lumps are fine.
    4. Rest the batter 5 minutes while heating a lightly greased pan over medium heat.
    5. Pour ¼ cup batter per pancake. Flip when bubbles form and edges look set, 2–3 min.
    6. Cook 1–2 min more until golden. Serve warm with syrup.
