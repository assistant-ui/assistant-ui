🥞 Fluffy Pancakes

Classic buttermilk-style pancakes, golden and light.

Prep 10 min · Cook 15 min
Servings 4

Ingredients

    Steps

    1. Whisk flour, sugar, baking powder and salt in a large bowl.
    2. In another bowl, whisk milk, eggs and melted butter.
    3. Pour wet into dry and stir until just combined — a few lumps are fine.
    4. Rest the batter 5 minutes while you heat a lightly greased pan over medium heat.
    5. Pour ¼ cup batter per pancake. Flip when bubbles form and edges look set, about 2 minutes.
    6. Cook another 1–2 minutes until golden. Serve warm with syrup or fruit.
